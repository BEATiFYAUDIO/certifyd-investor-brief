Problem

The creator economy runs on platforms creators do not control.

Creators publish everywhere, but the economic and identity rails are fragmented, opaque, and platform-owned. Attribution breaks, payment flows disappear behind intermediaries, split participants wait, and official provenance is hard to verify across the open web.

01

Ownership is separated from distribution.

Creators need broad distribution without surrendering their official commerce, identity, and IP context to a single platform.

02

Attribution is not payment-aware.

Credits, derivatives, collaborators, receipts, and payouts are usually separate systems that drift apart over time.

03

AI discovery needs reliable source context.

As AI discovery grows, creators need durable, machine-readable provenance and commercial truth attached to their works.
