Why This Matters

Creators need infrastructure they can own.

Creators depend on centralized platforms for audience access, publishing, discovery and payments. Certifyd moves those pieces closer to creators and operators, then connects them through a shared network.

The old model.

One platform hosts everything, pays for everything and controls the relationship.

The Certifyd model.

Creators and operators run their own nodes while Certifyd connects identity, discovery and commerce.

The upside.

Self-hosting is one advantage: creators can own more of their presence without leaving the network.
