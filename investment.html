Market Opportunity

Why now.

Creators are more dependent on centralized platforms at the same time that AI makes attribution, provenance and direct commerce more important. The market needs portable infrastructure, not another destination app.

Platform lock-in is expensive.

Audiences, payment flows, discovery and receipts are fragmented across systems creators do not control.

AI raises the value of provenance.

Source context, attribution, rights metadata and public-safe records become more valuable as content discovery becomes machine-assisted.

Direct commerce is expanding.

Creators, agencies, brands, educators, filmmakers, podcasters and communities need portable commerce infrastructure that can follow their audience.

Economic Engine

The economic engine is infrastructure participation.

Certifyd participates in economic activity flowing through creator-owned networks. Sales, unlocks, premium releases, collaborations, licensing, provider-node activity and campaigns can create infrastructure revenue.

Creator commerce.

Creators and partners bring paid access, releases, collaborations, licensing and campaigns into the network.

Certifyd rails.

Identity, publishing, payment routing, attribution, receipts, provenance and access context execute the flow.

Splits and payouts.

Commerce activity can resolve contributors, receipts, split context, remittance and public-safe records.

Provider nodes.

Operators expand the network without Certifyd managing every creator relationship centrally.

Network revenue.

Small infrastructure percentages across large creator-commerce volume create scalable upside.

White-label and enterprise deployments.

Premium creator commerce tooling.

Campaign and attribution infrastructure.

Settlement Layer

Why Bitcoin and Lightning matter.

Bitcoin and Lightning are strategic settlement infrastructure, not a crypto marketing layer. Payment rails can evolve, but Certifyd benefits from a global, neutral and instant settlement network today.

Instant global settlement.

Creators can move value without platform payout queues or geography-bound banking assumptions.

Programmable commerce flows.

Receipts, split execution, remittance logic and creator-controlled commerce can operate close to the transaction.

Neutral foundation.

Certifyd can support future rails while using Bitcoin and Lightning as the settlement base for direct creator commerce.
