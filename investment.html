The Opportunity

The current creator model becomes more expensive as it grows.

Traditional creator platforms centrally finance storage, delivery, publishing infrastructure, account systems and commercial operations. As more creators publish more work and attract more audience activity, the central operator absorbs more infrastructure and delivery cost.

Centralized model.

More creators create more centrally hosted content, more delivery demand and more operating cost for the platform.

Certifyd model.

Creators and providers can contribute publishing and hosting capacity, while Certifyd focuses on the shared services that connect and commercialize the network.

Certifyd does not eliminate infrastructure costs. It changes the structure of those costs by removing the requirement for one company to centrally host every creator catalog as the foundation of the business.

The Model

Creators operate the infrastructure. Certifyd connects the economy.

Certifyd Core is the downloadable creator node engine. Creators and operators use it to publish, host, manage and prove their work from infrastructure they control.

Why Now

The market is moving toward owned channels and verifiable commerce.

Creators, agencies, labels, educators, filmmakers and communities need commercial infrastructure that follows their audience instead of locking them into one platform.

Platform dependence is costly.

Audience access, payment flows and receipts remain fragmented across systems creators do not control.

AI increases the value of source context.

Attribution, rights metadata and public ownership records matter more as discovery becomes machine-assisted.

Direct commerce is expanding.

More creator categories need portable paid access, releases, campaigns and collaboration workflows.
