The Opportunity

Creator businesses need ownership, not another rented platform.

Traditional platforms make money from attention. Certifyd makes money from services and network participation around creator businesses.

Today's creator platforms control discovery, transactions and customer relationships. Certifyd helps creators own more of that business, then earns from optional creator services and commercial partner participation around the network.

Creators own the customer.

Certifyd helps creators keep more of the relationship they build.

Shared operating model.

Traditional platforms become more expensive as every creator grows. Certifyd shares more of the operating model with creators and partners.

The Model

Two sides. Multiple revenue streams.

Certifyd monetizes optional creator services and commercial partner participation. Core remains free; revenue expands as creators and partners use more network functions.

Creator Revenue

Optional services creators choose to use.

Registration, promotion, Fan / Discovery, Awards, Infrastructure Assist and future premium network functions.

Partner Revenue

Commercial access and participation.

Retail partners, strategic partnerships, developer / API access, enterprise services, licensing, white-label and distribution integrations.
